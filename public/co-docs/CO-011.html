Executive Summary

Since the start of TCC’s mobilization, BLHI has issued five revisions to the project master schedule. Each successive revision has pushed controls-related milestone dates later, compressed TCC’s available execution window, and increased the number of areas running concurrently under adverse conditions. The 7/1/26 site walk confirmed that multiple predecessor activities are past their scheduled completion dates with no work performed — including four AHUs unset at or past their scheduled dates and a critical framing wall that blocks installation of four control panels.

This change order presents three discrete cost items arising from these conditions, totaling $21,560.55. Detailed pricing basis for each item is included below. TCC reserves rights for additional costs beyond those quantified here, as noted in the Reservation of Rights section.

Item 1 — Part-Time Scheduler Cost

TCC engaged a part-time scheduler to track, analyze, and prepare formal written responses to recurring GC schedule revisions on this project. This cost is outside TCC’s original scope and was not included in TCC’s contract price. The frequency and volume of schedule revisions on this project is beyond what should be expected on a properly coordinated project and has imposed an ongoing administrative cost that TCC is entitled to recover.

The need for this line item exists entirely because of how this project has been managed above TCC’s tier — and that cost belongs with the parties responsible for it.

Item 3 — Predecessor & Access Delays

TCC’s 7/1/26 site walk confirmed that multiple predecessor activities are past their scheduled completion dates with no work performed in the field. TCC cannot begin or progress HVAC controls work in any area until predecessor work (AHU setting, drywall/framing, panel mounting locations) is complete. The conditions documented below directly prevent TCC from executing per the current schedule and represent delays imposed on TCC by others.

3A — AHU Predecessor Delays

Four air handling units are past their scheduled set dates as of the 7/1/26 site walk. TCC cannot begin controls wiring, connection, or programming for any AHU until the unit is set, powered, and accessible.

3B — Event D Wall — Critical Path Impact (Four Control Panels)

The wall designated to carry TCC’s Cooling Tower control panel and three AHU control panels in the Event D area was not framed as of the 7/1/26 site walk. TCC cannot mount any of these four panels, run conduit to panel locations, or begin wiring until this wall is constructed. These are not incidental panels — they represent a concentration of critical controls infrastructure for the building’s primary cooling and air handling systems.

Event D Control Panel Wall — Status as of 7/1/26 Wall for Cooling Tower control panel and AHU 5, AHU 7, AHU 8, and AHU 12 control panels: Not framed. No substrate exists for panel mounting. Zero conduit or panel work can proceed. This condition was documented and communicated to ECS/Blane Ivey via correspondence dated 7/1/26.

TCC’s site walk identified multiple GC schedule activities marked as “Actual” (complete) that are demonstrably not complete in the field. This pattern of inaccurate reporting masks the true predecessor status of TCC’s work and impairs TCC’s ability to plan resource deployment based on the issued schedule. Scope Limitation & Reservation of Rights

CO-011 is submitted for the three discrete impact categories identified above. This change order does not address, and is not intended to eliminate, settle, waive, or prejudice, any prior or pending change order requests, reserved rights, or future claims.

Approval of CO-011 shall not be construed as a waiver of or release from: (a) any rights or amounts pending under CO-001 through CO-009; (b) any rights reserved in TCC’s prior schedule impact reviews (dated 09 Mar 26, 06 Apr 26, 27 Apr 26, 28 May 26, and 15 Jun 26); (c) any claim for damage to TCC’s installed work by others, including removed J-hooks, panel brackets, cut communication cables, removed strut, and repeated roof penetration repair for which no approved change order has been received; (d) any claim for directed change in means and methods, including but not limited to any direction to install conduit on strut/hangers in lieu of drywall surfaces contrary to TCC’s submitted Schedule Durations Worksheet (4/21/25); or (e) any rights arising from future schedule revisions, liquidated damages assertions, or additional access denials. CO-011 is additive to all prior submittals and does not supersede, modify, or release any claim previously made or reserved by TCC.

Commercial Position

The conditions underlying this change order are documented in the GC’s own issued schedules (OPS 260427 and OPS 260615) and TCC’s own field observations on 7/1/26 — conditions that are not the result of any TCC performance issue, delay, or default. TCC’s original contract price was developed against a baseline scope of work performed in a defined sequence, within a defined execution window, against predecessor activities completed per the governing schedule. The cumulative effect of five schedule revisions, compressing the execution window by eight days, driving all eight areas later, and leaving critical predecessor work incomplete at or past its scheduled date, constitutes a material departure from the conditions under which TCC contracted.

TCC has continued to perform in good faith throughout these changes and has provided formal written documentation of each schedule revision’s impact. TCC reserves all rights for further cost and schedule impacts arising from additional revisions, predecessor delays, directed changes in means and methods, or conditions outside TCC’s control.
