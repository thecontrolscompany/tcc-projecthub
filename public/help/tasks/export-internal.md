# Internal Estimate Export

The internal export generates a detailed cost breakdown for internal review and project management.

## Generating the Internal Export

1. Open an estimate.
2. Click **Export Internal** (or the internal export action in the estimate toolbar).
3. The file is downloaded automatically.

## What's Included

The internal export includes:

- Full assembly-level cost detail per line item
- Labor hours and material cost separately
- Markups applied at each level
- Overhead items and equipment rentals
- Bond calculation
- Price snapshot dates (when each item was priced)

## Using the Internal Export

Common uses:

- Project management cost tracking
- Bid review and margin analysis
- Audit trail for estimate accuracy
